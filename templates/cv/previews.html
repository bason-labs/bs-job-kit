CV layouts

Grey bars stand in for text. Pick one with cvLayout in profile.yaml, or write your own (see README.md).

A · Classic single column

One column from top to bottom. Safest for job-site and HR systems that read CVs automatically.
cvLayout: classic

B · Sidebar

Left column for photo, contact, skills and links. The main column holds experience.
cvLayout: sidebar

C · Header band, two columns

A bold name band across the top, experience wide on the left, skills narrow on the right.
cvLayout: band

D · Modern timeline

A centred header and jobs on a vertical timeline. Feels more designed and is less dense.
cvLayout: timeline
